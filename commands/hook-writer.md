Generate categorized hook variations and a full short-form video script from the following brief, angle, or product description:

$ARGUMENTS

---

## Step 1 — Analyze the Input

Extract:
- **Topic / Product / Idea**: What is this about?
- **Target Audience**: Who is this for?
- **Core Value Proposition or Tension**: What makes this interesting or worth watching?
- **Desired Emotion**: Curiosity, fear, inspiration, humor, shock, etc.

---

## Step 2 — Hook Variations (10 total, 2 per category)

Generate exactly 10 hook lines grouped into the following 5 categories. Each hook must be a single punchy line (under 15 words) optimized for the first 3 seconds of a short-form video.

### Curiosity / Open Loop
Hooks that withhold information to force the viewer to keep watching.

### Controversy / Bold Claim
Hooks that challenge assumptions, say something provocative, or take an unpopular stance.

### Personal Story / Confession
Hooks that open with a vulnerable, relatable, or surprising personal moment.

### Stat / Proof / Credibility
Hooks that lead with a striking number, fact, or social proof.

### Humor / Pattern Interrupt
Hooks that are absurd, self-aware, or subvert expectations to stop the scroll.

---

## Step 3 — Recommended Hook

Pick the single strongest hook from the 10 above. Explain in 1–2 sentences why it will perform best for this specific audience and topic.

---

## Step 4 — Full Short-Form Video Script

Write a complete script using the recommended hook. Format:

**HOOK** (0–3s)
> [Spoken line. On-screen text if different.]

**PROBLEM / TENSION** (3–10s)
> [Establish why this matters. Agitate the pain or curiosity.]

**BODY** (10–45s)
> [Deliver the value: steps, story beats, reveal, or demonstration. Use short punchy sentences. Write for spoken delivery.]

**CTA / CLOSE** (last 3–5s)
> [One clear call to action or payoff line. No more than one ask.]

---

**Script Notes:**
- Tone: [match to audience and topic]
- Pacing: [fast/medium/slow]
- Visual suggestions: [2–3 brief ideas for on-screen text, cuts, or b-roll]
